# Beach picnic identities

The picnic now labels Button, Mabel, Pip and Pepper directly in the scene, with
matching names on their movable mats. The original star, leaf, paw and paw/star
symbols remain. Waiting cats align with their own starting mats and face the
player; their name labels follow them to their assigned mats on completion.
The beach picnic camera includes the waiting row above the bottom controls.
This applies to both saved v1 picnics and the current v2 arrangement.

Names use original cream/teal UI artwork generated by
`scripts/generate-cat-name-tags.py` from the existing licensed Nunito Sans font.
The four small PNGs are embedded in `cat_name_tags.inc`, sharing one immutable
unlit material per cat. No additional model, external font or runtime download
is introduced. Puzzle rules and saved progression are unchanged.

Validation uses the generated site on port 8080, build
`paws-0.1.0-b876da0525e1494e`. The focused
`scripts/picnic-identity-smoke.cjs` seeds a native-produced saved-game fixture,
then uses real pointer drags to clear baskets, place mats, undo a placement and
complete the picnic. WebGPU and forced WebGL2 passed without console errors.
The older v1 save also passed the full WebGPU placement and undo flow.
Reports and screenshots are under `build/evidence/picnic-identity/`.
Desktop, portrait and short-landscape screenshots are viewport emulation;
they do not establish physical mobile acceptance.

The portable scene regression passed, including checks that each waiting cat
aligns with its own authored mat and its name follows the completed placement.
