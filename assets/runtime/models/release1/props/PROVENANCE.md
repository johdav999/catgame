## Current bell assembly replacement, 14 October 2026

`bell_support`, `bell_leaf`, `bell_star` and `bell_paw` are now derivatives of
Tripo3D task `ec8e0434-564d-4789-853f-343823787558`, generated from an original
built-in ImageGen bell-rack reference (60 provider credits). Blender separates
the two-post frame and the three independently clickable bells, fits existing

maps with matte material factors. Exact original Paws leaf/star/paw geometry

the service tier and delivered dimensions are recorded separately.

## Original prop kit

Original Paws geometry, UVs, symbols, assemblies and Blender scripts authored for
this project on 11 October 2026 using Blender 3.5.1. No downloaded meshes or
engine demonstration assets were used. Geometry is project-authored work; this
record does not assign a new third-party license to it.

The approved garden-kit revision 7 supplies the wood, cloth, sage cloth, wicker
and stone materials. Its six-tile 1536 x 1024 atlas was generated using OpenAI's
built-in ImageGen on 10 October 2026. The original output, exact prompt and
rights context remain at `artifacts/model-jobs/garden-kit/2026-10-10/sources/`
and that job's `PROVENANCE.md`. Native 512 x 512 tiles are reused without upscale.
Generated color artwork is subject to the applicable generation service terms;
no CC0, scan provenance, third-party texture license or account-entitlement
certification is asserted. No new provider generation occurred in this task.

Roughness is independently authored linear scalar data. Neutral OpenGL tangent
normals leave relief to actual geometry. Neither channel is inferred from color
luminance. These images remain packed in the editable source and embedded in
the GLBs; metallic-roughness uses glTF G/B channels. Opaque water, simple painted
teal/cream, symbol geometry and small metal accents use solid materials because
additional images would not improve their intended readable appearance.

Unchanged legacy meshes keep their own provenance at
`assets/runtime/models/garden/NOTICE.md`; the fragment references their exact
hashes instead of duplicating or replacing them. Cat files are not modified.

Editable sources, material source identities, validation, observed corrections
and limitations: `artifacts/model-jobs/release1-props/2026-10-11/`.
