assets are included. Pip is a separate asset with its own provenance.

Editable sources, original generated pixels, prompt, transformations, rights
record and verification are retained in
`artifacts/model-jobs/garden-kit/2026-10-10/PROVENANCE.md` in the source repository.

meshes, authored 11 October 2026. Their script, packed review sources and hashes
are retained in `artifacts/shop-previews/2026-10-11/` in the source repository.
