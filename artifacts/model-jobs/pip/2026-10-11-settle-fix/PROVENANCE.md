# Provenance

Geometry, bind rig, UVs and materials derive unchanged from the repository's
accepted `2026-10-10-tripo-repair/pip-repaired-r8.blend`. The original supplied
Tripo source and its license/provenance remain in that job and `assets/Pip`.
No additional downloaded model, motion capture, texture or generated artwork is
used. Existing approved coat maps are reused for this animation-only revision.

The user's four screenshots establish the reported defect from different angles.
They were inspected as evidence and are not mesh or texture inputs.

New settle/rest body animation is authored by retained Blender Python scripts.
`merge_settle.py` is adapted from the local concurrent walk channel-merging tool,
with explicit preservation of current eyelid channels. Runtime hashes and source
layers are recorded in `asset-manifest.json` and the runtime manifest.
