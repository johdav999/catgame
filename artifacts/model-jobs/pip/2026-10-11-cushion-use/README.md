# Rejected cushion-use experiments

All `pip-cushion-r*.blend`/`.glb` and `r*-*.png` here are unaccepted candidates.
They were generated from the preserved repaired-r8 packed master, retaining Pip's
geometry, coat materials and canonical bind rig. None was promoted to runtime.
The source provenance remains in `../2026-10-10-tripo-repair/PROVENANCE.md`.

Revisions 1–7 had raised paws/body or script/pose defects. Revision 8 corrected
the script's repeated rejected tail override and reads as a compact C from above,
but remains crouched. Revision 9's side pose leaves raised paws/haunch and was
rejected. Renders and measurements remain for inspection; numerical ground
clearance is not visual or deformation acceptance. The independent connected-
joint repair is retained under `../2026-10-11-settle-fix/`.

Revision 10 uses that repaired packed master as a new base, with a smaller
horizontal bend. Its front render still reads as a sitting/crouched posture;
it was also rejected and never merged into runtime. The supported low rest from
the independent repair is the accepted runtime; a true sleeping curl remains open.

Revision 11 adds a head/neck rotation but tips the face upward, and revision 12
corrects the direction. Both retain a curled crouch silhouette rather than a
convincing sleeping pose. They were rejected; no candidate in this directory is
the authoritative runtime asset.
