# Sources and authorship

- Eadweard Muybridge, *Animal Locomotion*, plate 716, 1887; Boston Public Library
  digitization. [Source and rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Animal_locomotion._Plate_716_(Boston_Public_Library).jpg).
  Public-domain historical photograph; author died 1904. Native JPEG retained in
  `references/muybridge-716.jpg`. Walking frames 1–6 inspected for lifted forepaw,
  low reach, planted support and comparatively level trunk. Later gallop excluded.
  This reference is not shipped as a runtime texture.
- [Primary cat locomotion study, DOI 10.1152/jn.00524.2013](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/full/10.1152/jn.00524.2013):
  cross-check for lateral footfall sequence RH, RF, LH, LF (phase origin shifted
  to LH for the authored clip). No research figure reused.
- Existing `2026-10-10-tripo-repair/pip-repaired.blend`: approved Pip identity,
  canonical rig, maps and skin weights reused; source and rights records remain
  in that job. No new coat, mesh or generated character interpretation.
- Pose reconstruction authored here in `references/walk-spec.json` and
  `scripts/build_walk.py`. The photograph cannot supply exact 3D measurements;
  timing and reconstruction are an interpreted stylized walk, not motion capture.

Inspection sheets/videos are renders of the result, separated from photographic
input. No generated image is represented as anatomical evidence. Input checksums
and output checksums are recorded in the asset manifest.
